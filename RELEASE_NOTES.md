This prerelease publishes a candidate account of the degree-difference
determinant and affine slices of binary-form factorisation spaces, together
with exact local symbolic checks and an AI-readable evidence index.

The symbolic checker verifies selected explicit identities. In particular, it
constructs the manuscript's Sylvester matrix and verifies the full determinant
identity for all positive bidegrees with \(r+s\le4\), checks the base-point
sign convention for \(1\le r,s\le8\), and checks the explicit affine
parametrisation, inverse, Keller maps, Jacobians, three-point collision,
discriminant-coordinate conversion, and triple-root formulas. Ordinary and
optimized runs match the recorded transcript; a deliberately false
determinant expectation is rejected in both modes.

The checker does not verify the manuscript's all-degree determinant argument,
torsor and divisor-class proofs, cubic orbit classification, affine-bundle
arguments, global nonproperness proof, higher-degree obstruction, or
multifactor observation.

No independent external reproduction, external human review of the complete
manuscript, literature-based novelty determination, peer review, or
end-to-end formalisation is documented. This release invites issues,
counterexamples, and independent checks.
